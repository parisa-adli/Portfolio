# Keyboard reference notes

Sources: `docs/keyboard (1).webp` (low side view) and `docs/keyboard (2).webp` (3/4 top view). The `.jpg` named in the brief does not exist; the owner chose these two photos instead.

- **Layout size in the photos:** about 75%. It has a function row (F1–F12), a full alpha block, an inverted-T arrow cluster at the bottom right, and a 3×3 block of macro/art keys at the right edge. There is also a small display module in the bottom-right corner and a round knob/badge key at the top left. **The owner wants it full-sized on the site** (DESIGN.md §5.1): never trim rows.
- **Case:** translucent purple/blue acrylic with a frosted, glassy look. It glows from inside, strongest along the front edge and at the corners. There's a small branded wordmark in pink on the front lip (do not copy the brand).
- **Keycap profile:** sculpted, cylindrical-top keycaps of medium height, similar to Cherry/OEM, with shine-through side walls. The light leaks out under and around every cap.
- **Colorway:**
  - Caps: deep indigo/violet (about `#3B2F8F`–`#4B3FB0`) with white/lavender legends.
  - Secondary legends: small orange (`fn` layer) and cyan/pink accents.
  - Spacebar: solid coral/salmon (about `#FF5A6E`).
  - Background: near-black navy.
- **Lighting:** multicolour per-key RGB between the caps (cyan, green, yellow, orange, red, pink) plus a blue/violet underglow through the case.
- **Notable keys:**
  - Art keycaps with flowing orange/pink/yellow liquid-style graphics (`Enter`, `Backspace`, right `Ctrl`/`OP`).
  - Teal novelty caps on the left (`Caps`/`Shift` area), and a "GAME ON!" art cap.
  - A 3×3 block of icon caps with character art.
  - Arrow keys with coloured legends.
  - On the site these art slots become the **tech-stack keycaps** (React, Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind, Node, GraphQL), and the coral spacebar stays as the accent.
